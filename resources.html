Further Research

Explore the organisations, websites, and archives that preserve the history of the Kingston Avenue of Honour and the communities it represents.

Official Sources

Key Organisations

🌳

Kingston Avenue of Honour

Official website of the Kingston Friends of the Avenue — history, news, events, and the interactive tree map app with full records from Sentinels of Service.

kingstonavenueofhonour.org.au ↗
⚔️

Creswick-Smeaton RSL

The RSL sub-branch serving Creswick and Smeaton — supporting veterans and preserving the military history of the district including honour boards.

creswicksmeatonrsl.org.au ↗
🎖️

Australian War Memorial

Research individual service records, unit histories, photographs, and the Roll of Honour through the AWM's extensive online collections.

awm.gov.au ↗
⛏️

Buried Rivers of Gold

Explore the world's richest deep alluvial goldfield around Creswick, Allendale, Kingston and Smeaton — the mining heritage that shaped these communities.

buriedriversofgold.org ↗
📁

National Archives of Australia

Access digitised WWI service records and enlistment papers for individuals who served in the Australian Imperial Force.

naa.gov.au ↗
🏛️

Hepburn Shire Council

The local government authority — working with the Friends of the Avenue on tree maintenance, signage, and heritage preservation.

hepburn.vic.gov.au ↗

Get Involved or Share Information

If you have a family connection to the Avenue — photographs, letters, service records, or stories about a person commemorated by a tree — the Kingston Friends of the Avenue would love to hear from you.

Contact through kingstonavenueofhonour.org.au/contact

For RSL honour boards, visit creswicksmeatonrsl.org.au
