286 trees. 286 lives. One road through history.

Welcome to the former Shire of Creswick Avenue of Honour at Kingston. Throughout this website, we refer to it as the Kingston Avenue of Honour — an interactive digital journey honouring the men and women who served in the Great War, commemorated by Dutch Elm trees lining 2.9 kilometres of Kingston Road.
